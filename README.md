# Geo-Energy Radar — Real-Time US-Iran Oil & Geopolitics Tracker

> Real-time geopolitical and energy intelligence dashboard tracking US-Iran oil flows, maritime tanker routes, and conflict news.

### Tech Stack & Topics
`oil-market` | `geopolitics` | `news-tracker` | `dashboard` | `data-visualization`


An interactive real-time intelligence monitoring application providing situational awareness on geopolitical developments, Middle Eastern crude oil tanker corridors, and energy market impacts.

###  Tech Stack & Topics
`oil-market` `geopolitics` `news-tracker` `dashboard` `data-visualization`

---

###  Capabilities
- **Live News Aggregation:** Feeds and updates covering bilateral developments.
- **Maritime Energy Tracking:** Visualizing critical straits and supply chains.
- **Interactive UI:** Lightweight, responsive design for quick news scanning.
